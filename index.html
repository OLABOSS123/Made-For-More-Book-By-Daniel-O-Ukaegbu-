THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGES

Meet Daniel
O. Ukaegbu.

"You were created for more than your ambitions. You were created for God's purpose."

Daniel O. Ukaegbu is a servant of Jesus Christ called to the apostolic, prophetic, and evangelistic ministry.

He is the Founder and President of The Secret Place Ministry Worldwide, also known as Fulfilling Your Destiny International, founded in 2014 in Gusau, Zamfara State, Nigeria.

A trained journalist who turned to evangelism, Daniel is also a gifted writer, dramatist, worshipper, and singer with a passion for souls and intimacy with God.

In 2014, he left journalism and movie pursuits to answer God's call. Through ministry and writing, he seeks to inspire people to grow spiritually and discover God's purpose for their lives.
